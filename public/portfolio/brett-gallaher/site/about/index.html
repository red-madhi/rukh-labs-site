POSITIONING & METHOD

Analytics becomes useful when the whole chain holds.

My work sits between data engineering, semantic modeling, business logic, governance, and interaction design. The report is only the visible edge.

01

Frame the decision

Define users, questions, grain, terms, constraints, and what a truthful answer can support.

02

Engineer evidence

Separate source systems, inject known defects, create controls, and reconcile every transformation boundary.

03

Model deliberately

Choose star, snowflake, accumulating snapshot, role-playing date, or bridge patterns because the decision needs them.

04

Author reusable logic

Build explicit measures and calculation behavior with clear dependencies, format strings, tests, and performance notes.

05

Design for action

Use the simplest honest visual, preserve precise lookup paths, expose context, and make scenarios understandable.

06

Validate and disclose

Automate what the environment can prove and label every Desktop, service, identity, or deployment boundary that remains.
